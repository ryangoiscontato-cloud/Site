Can you explain quantum computing in simple terms?
Quantum computing harnesses quantum mechanics to process information in ways impossible for classical computers.

Think of it like this: a classical bit is a coin lying flat — either heads (1) or tails (0). A qubit is that same coin spinning in the air, being both at once. That's superposition.
